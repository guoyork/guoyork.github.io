About Me

我是北京大学计算机学院的五年级博士生，本科于2021年毕业于北京大学元培学院以及信息科学技术学院图灵班。我的导师是孔雨晴副教授，我的研究兴趣包括鲁棒信息聚合、机制设计以及大模型应用。

I am a fifth-year PhD student at the School of Computer Science in Peking University (2021-). I received my Bachelor's degree from Yuanpei College (Turing Class), Peking University in 2021. I am advised by Prof. Yuqing Kong and my research interests are Robust Information Aggregation, Mechanism Design and Application of Large Language Models.
